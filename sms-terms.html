NeverMissALead, SMS Terms

Last updated August 2026

NeverMissALead is a missed-call text-back service used by property-service contractors (roofing, paving, landscaping, window installation) to automatically reply to a customer when a call to the business goes unanswered.

Consent

A customer is enrolled in this messaging program only after they call the contractor's business phone number. Calling that number is the opt-in: it signals the customer wants a follow-up, so the business's automated system sends one text reply to continue that same conversation, then further texts only while that inquiry is open (for example, while scheduling a site visit).

Message content and frequency

Messages relate only to the customer's own inquiry: confirming details, asking qualifying questions, and scheduling an appointment. No marketing or promotional messages are sent. Frequency depends on the conversation and normally ends once an appointment is booked or the customer stops replying.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Data use

Conversation content and phone numbers are used only to respond to and schedule the customer's service request. They are not sold or shared with third parties, and conversation history is retained for 24 hours before it's automatically deleted.
